`

includeFilter://m:GET
includeFilter:///api/
excludeFilter:///api/internal/
`

# 用户规则兼容回归：多个 includeFilter 为 OR；裸 webpack-hmr 自动补 `/`；
# `*/resource/` 作为目录前缀匹配其子路径；任一 excludeFilter 仍优先排除。
line`
life-rule.local
reqHeaders://X-Life-Rule=matched
includeFilter://*/h5/fulfillment-fusion/
includeFilter://*/resource/
includeFilter://webpack-hmr
excludeFilter://*/life/
`
